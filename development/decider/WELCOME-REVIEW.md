# Welcome page and examples - development/test release

Version 1.17. The personal decision journey retains the dark green/lime design and original brand promise. The example collection uses cream reading surfaces and blue actions. Example answers stay labelled through the calculator, result and Explore. A separate Start my own decision route begins fresh.

24 examples: four public decisions, five business, five other money, ten happiness. The mixed selection includes one of each category. Topic filters and a remembered skip option are included. Each card shows the calculated answer under its example assumptions. Heathrow is an investor construction-commitment case rather than a universal policy verdict.

Checks: existing calculation and precision tests; all 24 results and 24 variations; 500 mixed selections; category filters; independent 110-digit checks; browser checks for all editable examples, contact return, offline use, skip preferences, and 320/390/1024px layouts. Mathematics and tracking settings are unchanged.

The source lives in development/decider/src on decider-development. The built preview is published separately at /decider-test/. The public /sigfi/ release is not replaced by this update.
